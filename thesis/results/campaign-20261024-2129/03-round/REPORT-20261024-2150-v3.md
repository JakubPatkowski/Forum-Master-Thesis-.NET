## Statistical tests (per-repeat values; the repeat is the experimental unit)
Primary test: exact two-sided Mann-Whitney U with Holm-Bonferroni adjustment over each family of metrics (`scripts/ab_stats.py`); Cliff's δ = effect size (positive = B larger; |δ| ≥ 0.474 large); Welch's t-test (two-sided) and its own Holm-adjusted p are the parametric cross-check. Verdicts: *distinct* = MW Holm p < 0.05; *raw-only* = only the unadjusted MW p < 0.05 (suggestive); *underpowered* = even complete separation of the samples could not pass Holm at this n (with 5 vs 5 the smallest exact MW p is 0.0079, × 14 tests > 0.05 — N ≥ 10 per side is needed for a Holm-level verdict); *no difference detected* ≠ equal (no equivalence test is run); *n<3* = too few repeats.

## Reading guide and known limits
See `thesis/results/AB-COMPARISON-v1.md` for the methodology, the fairness invariants and the deviation register (§6.2) that every number in this report carries — in particular D1 (A's default adapter is an idealized SPA client; `--real-spa` rounds measure the real one), D3 (read journeys are anonymous unless `--auth-reads`), D8 (platform-default GC policies behind the memory rows), D12 (B's J4 waits for its upload poll — hence the *core* latency rows) and D14 (A's realtime receipt floor is its outbox poll).
